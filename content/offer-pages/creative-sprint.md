### Three milestones. No surprises.

**Day 1–3: Research + Concept**

**Day 3–10: Build**
Based on your feedback, I build the full creative kit — hooks, concepts, landing copy, testing plan. You see a draft at Day 10.

## SECTION 9: FAQ

**How is this different from hiring an agency?**
Agencies give you outputs. I give you outputs plus the brief, the testing plan, and the structure to keep building. You keep the system, not just the deliverables.

**What if I don't like the creative?**
You get 2 revision rounds. Each round is one consolidated set of feedback. I adjust and deliver. If after 2 rounds it's still not right, we discuss what's off — but in practice, the structured brief process catches misalignment at Day 3, not Day 14.

**Do you use AI?**
AI is a tool in my process, not the process itself. I use it to accelerate research and draft generation, but every deliverable is reviewed, edited, and QA'd by me. You're hiring my judgment and experience, not a prompt.

**What do you need from me?**
A URL, product details, brand guidelines (if any), past winners/losers, and ~2 hours of your time for intake and reviews. That's it.

**What happens after the sprint?**
If the sprint goes well and you want ongoing creative iteration, I offer a monthly retainer (4 new angles/month + refresh kit + QA). But there's zero pressure — the sprint is complete and useful on its own.
