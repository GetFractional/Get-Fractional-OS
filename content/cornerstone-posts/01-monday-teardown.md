## LinkedIn Post Copy

Why your hooks all sound the same (and what to do instead):

I review DTC ads every week. The #1 pattern I see killing performance?

Every hook starts from the same angle.

"Tired of [problem]?"
"What if you could [benefit]?"
"Introducing [product]."

Same structure. Same emotional entry point. Over and over.

Your audience sees 300+ ads a day. If your first 3 seconds sound like everyone else's first 3 seconds, you've already lost.

Here's the fix:

Instead of writing 10 hooks from one angle, write 1 hook from each of these 5 angles:

- Pain (the problem they feel right now)
- Desire (the outcome they actually want)
- Objection (the reason they haven't bought yet)
- Proof (what someone like them experienced)
- Curiosity (something they didn't expect)

One product. Five entry points. Five different reasons to stop scrolling.

The brands that beat creative fatigue don't write more ads.

They write ads that come at the problem from more directions.
