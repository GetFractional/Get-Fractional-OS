## LinkedIn Post Copy

I kept seeing the same 3 problems kill creative projects:

1. The brief was a vibe, not a document. Everyone had a different idea of what "good" looked like. The creative team was guessing.

2. The scope expanded until nobody remembered the original goal. "Just one more round" turned into months of back-and-forth.

3. There was no plan for what to test. The work shipped, the team moved on, and nobody learned anything.

I watched this happen at agencies. I watched it happen with freelancers. I watched founders try to do it themselves between 50 other priorities.

The problem wasn't talent. Every one of these projects had smart people working on them.

The problem was structure.

So I productized the fix.

14 days. Clear scope. A kit you keep.

Angles, hooks, ad concepts, landing copy, and a testing plan — built from your URL and your actual ICP.

I called it the Creative Sprint.

It's not magic. It's a process that removes the 3 things that usually go wrong.

If you're spending on paid social and your creative feels stale, comment "SPRINT" and I'll reply with the Fit Check details.
