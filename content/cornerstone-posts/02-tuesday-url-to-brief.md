## LinkedIn Post Copy

Every campaign I build starts the same way.

I open the client's URL. That's it. No 6-week discovery. No 40-page brand questionnaire.

Just the URL.

Here are the 3 things I pull from any product page in the first 30 minutes:

1. Who they're actually selling to (not who they think they're selling to). The language on the page tells you. Benefit framing, proof placement, objection handling — it all reveals the real ICP.

2. The objections hiding in their copy. If your FAQ section answers "Is this safe?" and "Does it really work?" — those are your top objections. Your ads should be built around them, not around features.

3. The angle gaps. Most brands hammer one message: "Our product is better." But their own page usually contains 3-4 untapped angles buried in testimonials, ingredient lists, or comparison sections.

This takes 30 minutes.

From here, I build the ICP brief, the objection map, and the first angle set.

That's the starting point for the whole creative kit — hooks, concepts, landing copy, testing plan.

It all starts with the URL.
