## LinkedIn Post Copy

People ask what they actually get from a 14-day Creative Sprint.

Here's the exact output:

- 1 Brand/ICP brief (who you're really selling to)
- 1 Objection map (5-7 reasons people don't buy, with rebuttal angles)
- 5 ad angles (each approaching your product from a different direction)
- 22 hooks (tailored to your product across all 5 angles)
- 15 ad concepts (copy, structure, and visual direction)
- 1 landing page copy kit (headline stack, sections, CTAs)
- 1 testing plan (naming conventions, test matrix, success criteria)

All built from your URL, your product, your actual customer.

What this is: a complete creative input kit. Everything a designer or media buyer needs to start testing.

What this isn't: a guarantee. I don't promise ROAS numbers or revenue. Nobody honest does.

What I promise is that you'll stop guessing what to run and start testing with a clear plan.

Two revision rounds. Three milestones. 14 days.

You keep the entire kit.
