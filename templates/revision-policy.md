## How Revisions Work

Every Creative Sprint includes **2 revision rounds**. Here's exactly how they work so there are no surprises.

---

### The 3 Rounds

| Round | What Happens | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Round 0 (Initial Delivery)** | I deliver the full creative kit — brief, angles, hooks, concepts, LP copy, testing plan. | Day 10 |
| **Round 1 (First Revision)** | You submit consolidated feedback. I implement changes in one pass. | Day 10–12 |
| **Round 2 (Final Revision)** | You submit final consolidated feedback. I implement and deliver the final kit. | Day 12–14 |

---

### The Rules

**1. Consolidated feedback only.**

Each revision round = ONE set of feedback. Not drips. Not "one more thing" emails over 3 days.

Submit all your feedback in a single document, message, or marked-up PDF. I consolidate and implement in one pass.

**Why:** Piecemeal feedback creates scope creep, version confusion, and delays. One consolidated round keeps the sprint on track.

**2. Feedback must be actionable.**

Good feedback: "The third hook feels too aggressive for our brand. Can we soften the tone while keeping the objection angle?"

Not useful: "I don't love it." / "Can you make it pop more?" / "Something feels off."

If feedback isn't actionable, I'll ask a clarifying question before implementing. This uses time but doesn't count as a round.

**3. 48-hour feedback window.**

You have 48 hours to submit feedback after each delivery milestone.

If feedback isn't received within 48 hours, the sprint pauses. See Scope Agreement Section 5 (Pause and Requeue Policy) for details.

**4. Scope stays fixed.**

Revision rounds are for refining the deliverables defined in the Scope Agreement.

Revisions do NOT include:
- Adding new deliverable types not in the original scope
- Expanding to additional ICPs or products
- Requesting net-new concepts beyond the original count
- Design production changes (I deliver copy and direction, not final assets)

If you want to expand scope, we can discuss an add-on or a follow-up sprint.

### Additional Revision Rounds

Need more than 2 rounds? It happens. Here's how it works:

- **Cost:** $1,000–$2,000 per additional round (depends on scope of changes)
- **Timeline:** Adds 3–5 business days per round
- **Process:** Same rules — consolidated feedback, one implementation pass

I'll quote the add-on before any work begins. No surprises.

---

### Tips for Great Feedback

1. **Be specific.** Point to the exact hook, concept, or section. "Hook 7 on page 3" is better than "one of the hooks."
2. **Explain the why.** "This doesn't match our brand voice because we never use exclamation points" helps me fix it right. "I don't like this" doesn't.
3. **Prioritize.** If you have 10 pieces of feedback, flag the top 3. I'll address everything, but knowing your priorities helps me make the right tradeoffs.
4. **Reference the brief.** If something feels off-brand, check the ICP brief first. Sometimes the disconnect is in the brief (easy to fix early), not the creative.

---

### What Happens After Round 2

After Round 2, the sprint is complete. You receive the final kit and own all deliverables per the Scope Agreement (Section 8).

If you want ongoing creative support after the sprint — monthly angles, hook refreshes, campaign iteration — I offer a retainer option. But the sprint is complete and useful on its own. Zero pressure.
