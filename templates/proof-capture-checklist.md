# Proof Capture Checklist

> Use during and after every sprint to capture proof objects for marketing.
> Proof is the #1 asset for selling the next sprint. Capture it systematically.

---

## CAPTURE POINTS

### At Sprint Kickoff (Day 1)
- [ ] Screenshot the intake form (anonymize if needed)
- [ ] Note the client's starting situation in 1-2 sentences
- [ ] Record what they said their biggest challenge was (exact words if possible)

### At Day 3 Concept Review
- [ ] Note client's initial reaction to the ICP brief
- [ ] Capture any "I never thought of it that way" moments
- [ ] Screenshot any positive Slack/email messages
- [ ] Count deliverables produced so far: ___ angles identified

### At Day 10 Draft Review
- [ ] Count full deliverables: ___ angles, ___ hooks, ___ concepts
- [ ] Note client feedback on draft quality
- [ ] Capture any quotes about speed or thoroughness
- [ ] Screenshot the deliverable package table of contents

### At Day 14 Final Delivery
- [ ] Final deliverable counts: ___ angles, ___ hooks, ___ concepts, LP kit (Y/N), testing plan (Y/N)
- [ ] Timeline: Delivered on time? Y/N — if delayed, by how many days and why?
- [ ] Client satisfaction rating (ask directly): ___/5
- [ ] Ask for a testimonial quote: "Would you mind sharing a sentence about the experience?"
- [ ] Ask about permission level upgrade: "Would you be open to a case study?"

### Post-Sprint (1-2 weeks after)
- [ ] Follow up: "Have you started testing any of the creative?"
- [ ] Capture any early results (even anecdotal)
- [ ] Ask: "What was the most valuable part of the sprint?"
- [ ] Note if they've referred anyone or mentioned you publicly

---

## PROOF OBJECT TYPES TO BUILD

From each sprint, aim to extract at least 3 of these:

| Type | Example | Use In |
|---|---|---|
| **Deliverable Count** | "5 angles, 22 hooks, 15 concepts in 14 days" | LinkedIn posts, offer page |
| **Timeline Proof** | "From URL to full creative kit in 14 days" | Offer page, case study |
| **Client Quote** | "I've never gotten this level of clarity from an agency" | Testimonial sections |
| **Process Demo** | "Turned 1 product URL into 5 distinct angles" | Tuesday URL-to-Brief posts |
| **Result Metric** | "Client launched 3 tests within a week of delivery" | Wednesday proof posts |
| **Before/After** | "Before: 1 angle, same hook. After: 5 angles, 22 hooks" | Carousel content |

---

## STORAGE

Save all proof objects in:
1. **Airtable** → Proof Objects table (with permission level noted)
2. **Google Drive** → "Proof Library" folder → subfolder per client
3. Tag with permission level: Full Public / Anonymized / Process Only

**Never publish a proof object above the client's permission level.**
