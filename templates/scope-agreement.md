### 2. SCOPE OF WORK

**What's included:**

- More than 2 products per sprint

### 4. REVISION POLICY

**Included:** 2 revision rounds.

**Rules:**
1. All feedback for each round must be submitted as ONE consolidated document or message. Piecemeal feedback across multiple messages does not constitute a revision round.
2. Provider consolidates and implements feedback in one pass per round.
3. Round 0 = initial delivery. Round 1 = first revision. Round 2 = final revision.

**Additional revision rounds:** Available at $1,000–$2,000 per round, depending on scope, with a revised timeline.

### 5. PAUSE AND REQUEUE POLICY

Sprint timelines assume client feedback within **48 hours** of each review milestone (Day 3 Concept Review, Day 10 Draft Review).

If no consolidated feedback is received within 48 hours of a review milestone:

- The sprint will be **paused**.
- Provider will notify Client: "Your sprint is paused. When you're ready with consolidated feedback, we'll schedule you into the next available production slot."
- Provider may begin other engagements during the pause period.
- Resumed sprints are scheduled at the **next available production slot**, which may not be immediate.
- Paused sprints do not expire.
- The original delivery date will be adjusted to reflect the pause duration plus scheduling availability.
